**Algorithmic Enclosure Mechanisms:**
1. **Semantic Filters:** These are algorithms that analyze data streams in real-time to detect and suppress content that violates predefined ideological guidelines. The computational complexity of these filters is typically O(n log n) for large datasets, where n is the number of data points.
2. **Telemetry Harvesting:** This involves collecting metadata and usage patterns to build profiles and predict behavior. The data throughput required for effective telemetry is often in the range of terabytes per second, necessitating robust network infrastructure.

**Operational Perimeter:**
The operational perimeter of a self-sustaining offline data fortress is defined by its ability to operate independently of external networks. This requires:

1. **Energy Independence:** A reliable power source, such as solar or wind, to ensure continuous operation.
2. **Security Measures:** Robust encryption and access controls to prevent unauthorized data breaches.
3. **Redundancy:** Multiple layers of backup systems to mitigate hardware failures.
