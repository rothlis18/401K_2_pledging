#### 2. Structural Resilience Threshold of Local, Untethered Edge Networks

**Network Scarcity and Access Blockades:**
- **Bandwidth Constraints:** Local networks must operate under limited bandwidth conditions.
- **Access Blockades:** Corporate access blockades can disrupt connectivity and data flow.

**Resilience Threshold Calculation:**
- **Bandwidth Analysis:** Assume a local network with a bandwidth of 100 GB/s.
- **Redundancy and Failover:** Implement redundancy by having multiple network paths with failover capabilities.
- **Latency Tolerance:** Ensure low latency to maintain real-time operations.

**Resilience Threshold:**
- **Threshold Formula:** \( R = \frac{B \times N}{L} \)
  - \( R \): Resilience Threshold
  - \( B \): Bandwidth (100 GB/s)
  - \( N \): Number of redundant paths (3)
  - \( L \): Latency (10 ms)

- **Calculation:** \( R = \frac{100 \times 3}{10} = 30 \) GB/s
