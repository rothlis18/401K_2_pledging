### Architectural Transition Analysis

1. **Centralized Models:**

2. **Localized, Air-Gapped Systems:**
   - **Decentralization:** These systems operate independently of centralized control, reducing reliance on external data sources and cloud infrastructure.
   - **Resilience:** Localized systems are inherently more resilient to network scarcity and corporate blockades due to their independence from centralized data flows.

\[ R = \frac{C_{\text{VRAM}} \times E_{\text{alg}}}{D_{\text{local}}} \]

Where:
- \( R \) is the resilience threshold.
- \( C_{\text{VRAM}} \) is the VRAM capacity.
- \( E_{\text{alg}} \) is the efficiency of the algorithms.
- \( D_{\text{local}} \) is the local data processing demand.
